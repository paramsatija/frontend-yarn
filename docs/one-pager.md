## What It Is

YARN is a continuously learning ecosystem where every interaction strengthens the collective intelligence of the platform. It combines:

- **Institutional-grade data** across capital, legal, enterprise, governance, and treasury
- **Cryptographic attestation** on every data point
- **Private autonomous execution** that keeps your capital under your control
- **A self-reinforcing evidence engine** where every action improves every future action

## The Core Product: Private Autonomous Portfolio Execution

Deploy your own **YRN Execution Node** to your secure environment. Connect your exchange APIs. Define your risk parameters. The engine autonomously manages your portfolio — harvesting profits, reallocating capital, reducing risk — all within your predefined constraints.

**Your capital. Your keys. Your infrastructure. Your strategy.**

YARN supplies the intelligence. You control the execution.
